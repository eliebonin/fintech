# Market evidence, competition, pricing confidence, and discovery plan

## Purpose and evidence standard

This paper separates what is supported by public evidence from Fortem's commercial hypotheses. It should be read with the module briefs, not as a substitute for direct customer discovery.

Public vendor sites usually do not disclose institutional contract prices. Therefore the annual contract value (ACV) and win-probability figures below are **planning ranges**, not market facts. They are intentionally labelled with confidence rather than dressed up as sourced benchmarks.

## Market context

The addressable market is substantial but not homogeneous. EFAMA reports 1,380 UCITS management companies and 3,349 AIFMs in the EU, with further managers in the UK, Switzerland, Norway, and other European markets. [EFAMA Asset Management Report 2025](https://www.efama.org/sites/default/files/files/asset-management-report-2025-v3_0.pdf) The FCA describes £12.3tn of mainstream and £2tn of alternative assets managed by UK asset managers. [FCA, April 2025](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/rules-investment-managers-be-reformed-support-growth)

This does not mean every manager is a prospect. The initial ICP is a UK/EU manager with roughly £2bn–£20bn AUM, an open-ended fund or mandate business, meaningful derivatives or structured exposure, fragmented operating data, and a current control trigger.

## Demand evidence

### Valuation and data provenance

ESMA's valuation supervisory action identified scope for improvement in valuation policies and procedures, stressed-market valuation, independence, third-party valuers, and early valuation-error detection. This supports a control/evidence proposition, not a promise to take responsibility for fair value. [ESMA valuation findings](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-report-points-out-improvements-needed-supervision-asset-valuation)

### UCITS/AIF controls and liquidity

ESMA liquidity-management-tool guidelines cover selection and calibration of gates, notice periods, redemption fees, swing pricing, dual pricing, anti-dilution levies, and redemption in kind. [ESMA LMT Guidelines, 2026](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-03/ESMA34-671404336-1364_Guidelines_on_liquidity_management_tools_of_UCITS_and_open-ended_AIFs.pdf) FundApps' product page independently demonstrates that established buyers accept daily UCITS/AIFMD monitoring as a discrete service. [FundApps](https://www.fundapps.co/ucits-aifmd-monitoring)

### OTC and structured products

UCITS Article 51 requires a risk-management process and accurate, independent OTC-derivative valuation. [ESMA UCITS Article 51](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/ucits/article-51) FpML covers execution, confirmation, amendments, novations, terminations, cashflow matching, and portfolio reconciliation, demonstrating the depth of the lifecycle problem. [ISDA FpML](https://www.isda.org/isda-solutions-infohub/fpml/)

### Portfolio diagnostics

MSCI, Aladdin, and FactSet all sell portfolio risk, scenario, attribution, and multi-asset views. That validates demand, but also means Fortem needs a narrower differentiated offer. [MSCI](https://www.msci.com/data-and-analytics/portfolio-management/barra-portfolio-manager), [Aladdin](https://www.blackrock.com/aladdin/platforms/products/aladdin-risk), [FactSet](https://www.factset.com/solutions/portfolio-analytics)

## Commercial hypotheses

### Pricing bands

| Module | Target initial ACV | Typical onboarding | Confidence | Why this is a hypothesis |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Valuation control | £125k–300k | £100k–300k | Low–medium | Enterprise valuation/data vendors use quote-based pricing; scope varies sharply by sources, funds, and integration. |
| UCITS/AIF controls | £175k–400k | £150k–400k | Medium | Compliance software has clear recurring value, but maintained regulatory-content economics make direct comparison difficult. |
| OTC lifecycle | £150k–300k | £100k–250k | Medium | Strong product differentiation but implementation scope depends on confirmation formats and active contract count. |
| Onboarding/corporate actions | £75k–200k | £50k–200k | Low | This competes most directly with incumbent EDM/data-vendor contracts and is rarely bought alone. |
| Diagnostics | £60k–150k initially | £25k–100k | Medium | Start managed; move upmarket only after outputs reconcile and become embedded in client workflow. |

These ranges assume a controlled module deployed into existing systems. They do not include third-party market-data licence fees, major data remediation, full OMS replacement, or outsourced fund administration.

### Probability model

| Opportunity state | Realistic 12-month close probability | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Named cold account | 2–8% | No verified pain, sponsor, timeline, or budget. |
| Discovery-qualified account | 10–20% | Clear problem but no funded project or agreed proof. |
| Sponsored design-partner opportunity | 25–45% | Buyer sponsor, defined use case, committed data access, budget path, and success criteria. |
| Procurement/contracting | 55–75% | Commercial and security process started; still exposed to budget, legal, and incumbent objections. |

The module-specific differences in the briefs apply only to the third row. A generic probability that ignores qualification state is misleading.

## Minimum proof before product investment

Run 15–20 structured discovery conversations before committing to a broad build:

| Segment | Interviews | Primary question |
|---|---:|---|
| UCITS ManCo CRO/Conducting Officer | 4–5 | Which controls, LMT evidence, and depositary interactions are currently painful enough to fund? |
| COO / Head of Fund Operations | 4–5 | Which valuation, instrument, corporate-action, or administrator reconciliations consume staff time or create audit risk? |
| Derivatives / structured-product operations lead | 3–4 | Which lifecycle and confirmation exceptions are still manual, and what does one error cost? |
| CIO / portfolio-construction lead | 3–4 | Which diagnostics change a decision, and which existing risk outputs are distrusted or too slow? |
| Fund administrator / depositary / implementation partner | 2–3 | Which evidence packs, data interfaces, and handoffs would materially reduce operational friction? |

For each interview, capture: current process, incumbent tools, source data, annual external/internal spend, trigger event, buying committee, required integrations, deployment constraints, willingness-to-pay range, and a concrete proof-of-value dataset.

## Go / no-go gates

Advance a module only when all are true:

1. Three design partners describe the same expensive or risky workflow in materially similar terms.
2. At least two will provide anonymised/historic data for reconciliation.
3. At least one identifies a credible annual budget above £100k excluding third-party data licences.
4. Fortem can demonstrate a measurable outcome in eight to twelve weeks without replacing a core system.
5. The module has an advantage that an incumbent cannot easily replicate through configuration alone.

The most promising early thesis remains: **OTC/structured lifecycle plus valuation-control evidence**, with UCITS/liquidity controls as the regulated expansion path.
