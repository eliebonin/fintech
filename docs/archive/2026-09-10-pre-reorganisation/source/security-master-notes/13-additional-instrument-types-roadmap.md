# Additional instrument types roadmap

## Scope

The initial asset coverage is broad, but four product families deserve explicit future terms models. They are economically distinct and should not be forced into generic swaps, bonds, or options merely to reduce table count.

The design rule remains: one canonical economic instrument plus a focused terms extension where the product's economics require it. Reuse common dates, schedules, relationships, entities, market data, and trade lifecycle services.

## 1. Credit derivatives

Credit-default swaps have credit-event, reference-entity, settlement, and default-loss mechanics that go beyond ordinary swap legs.

```text
credit_default_swap_terms
  instrument_id
  protection_buyer / protection_seller
  reference_entity_org_id
  reference_obligation_instrument_id    -- nullable
  notional_currency
  fixed_coupon
  upfront_fee
  maturity_date
  credit_event_terms
  settlement_method                     -- AUCTION / PHYSICAL / CASH
```

Credit-index products require a non-tradeable `credit_index` reference object with series, version, and dated constituent snapshots. Tranches add attachment and exhaustion points. A CDS basket should use dated constituents/weights, not payoff composition.

```text
credit_index
  name, family, series, version

credit_index_constituent_snapshot
  credit_index_id, as_of_date, reference_entity_org_id, weight

credit_index_tranche_terms
  credit_index_id, attachment_point, exhaustion_point
```

Relevant future variants are single-name CDS, CDS index, index tranche, basket/Nth-to-default, loan CDS, mortgage/CMBS CDS, and CDS options.

## 2. Money-market instruments and financing

Different products belong in different structures:

| Product | Representation |
|---|---|
| Treasury bill, commercial paper, certificate of deposit | Zero-coupon or short-dated `BOND` instrument. |
| Term deposit | Economic `MONEY_MARKET_DEPOSIT` contract. |
| Repo/reverse repo | Dedicated `REPO` financing contract. |
| Securities lending/borrowing | Dedicated `SECURITIES_LENDING` financing contract. |
| FRA | Rates derivative, below. |

```text
money_market_deposit_terms
  instrument_id, currency, principal, start_date, maturity_date,
  rate, day_count, payment convention

repo_terms
  instrument_id, near_date, far_date, repo_rate, cash_currency,
  haircut, open_term_flag, tri_party_agent_org_id

repo_collateral
  repo_id, collateral_reference_node_id, quantity/market_value,
  effective_from, effective_to

securities_lending_terms
  instrument_id, loaned_reference_node_id, fee_or_rebate,
  collateral_type, open_term/recall terms
```

Collateral must not be stored as `instrument_composition`: it can be substituted during the repo or loan lifecycle. Portfolio-level collateral movements and margin operations are later trade/collateral-domain concerns.

## 3. Interest-rate derivatives beyond swaps

Existing `swap_terms`, `swap_leg`, schedules, rate-index nodes, curve snapshots, and volatility surfaces are reusable. Add only product-specific terms:

| Product | Focused extension |
|---|---|
| FRA | `fra_terms`: one forward period, rate index, fixing/settlement, agreed rate. |
| Cap/floor | `cap_floor_terms`: rate index, strike(s), cap/floor direction, schedule. |
| Swaption | `swaption_terms`: exercise terms and an underlying non-tradeable swap template. |
| Inflation swap | Generic swap legs plus `inflation_leg_terms`: inflation index, lag, interpolation. |
| Cross-currency swap | Generic swap legs plus initial/final exchanges and FX-reset terms. |

```text
swap_template
  non-tradeable reusable swap economics

swaption_terms
  instrument_id
  underlying_swap_template_id
  exercise_style                -- EUROPEAN / BERMUDAN / AMERICAN
  exercise_schedule
  settlement_method             -- CASH / PHYSICAL
```

The swap template is analogous to a future family: it represents reusable underlying economics, while each tradeable swaption remains a distinct economic instrument.

## 4. Convertibles, warrants, and rights

These are separate security types, even though their economics overlap with bonds or options.

```text
convertible_bond_terms
  instrument_id
  underlying_equity_node_id
  conversion_ratio
  conversion_window
  conversion_price / reset rules
  issuer_call / holder_put terms
  anti_dilution rules

warrant_terms
  instrument_id
  underlying_reference_node_id
  exercise terms
  warrant_ratio
  issuer_org_id
  settlement terms

rights_terms
  instrument_id
  corporate_action_id
  underlying_instrument_node_id
  entitlement_ratio
  subscription_price
  subscription_period
```

A convertible bond has `bond_terms` plus `convertible_bond_terms`. A warrant is a distinct issued security with option-like economics. A right is normally a temporary tradeable security generated by a rights corporate action and must link to that event.

## Implementation order

Prioritise only according to actual business scope:

1. Rate extensions: FRA, cap/floor, swaption, inflation/cross-currency additions.
2. Convertibles, warrants, and rights.
3. Credit-default swaps and credit-index definitions.
4. Repos and securities lending when financing activity enters scope.

FpML is a useful semantic and integration reference for all four domains, but its XML structures must be translated to the canonical relational model at the platform boundary.
