# Bond booking alpha

**Status:** Bounded booking alpha implemented, activated locally and verified with affected regressions  
**Owner:** Product and engineering  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

The bond ticket must book nominal exposure and explicit settlement economics, not pretend that a bond is a share. This bounded implementation reuses governed instrument terms, convention approvals, product booking, annotations, history snapshots and idempotency. It does not introduce a general coupon calculator or valuation engine.

## Research and selected scope

Sources opened and read on 2026-10-02:

- [FINRA bond guidance](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/bonds) explains percentage-of-par quotes, accrued interest and common OTC execution. Project decision: nominal quantity with PCT_OF_PAR and divisor 100; listed and OTC are separate execution routes, not inferred from an ISIN or listing's existence.
- [ICMA 2025 repo guide, glossary](https://www.icmagroup.org/assets/ERCC-Guide-to-Best-Practice-March-2025.pdf) distinguishes clean price, accrued interest and dirty price. Its repo collateral policies do not govern this cash ticket. Project decision: record the executed quote's CLEAN/DIRTY basis and the total signed accrued-interest currency amount separately, and derive the settlement consideration without double counting.
- [UK DMO gilt guidance](https://www.dmo.gov.uk/responsibilities/gilt-market/about-gilts/) shows that entitlement depends on settlement date, accrued interest can be negative in ex-dividend periods, and final ex-dividend settlement restrictions exist. Project decision: accept signed sourced accrued interest, including explicit zero; do not guess an accrual amount, coupon entitlement or settlement eligibility from coupon rate alone.
- [FINRA settlement-cycle guidance](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/understanding-settlement-cycles), updated May 2026, describes US T+1. This is not a universal bond-market rule. Project decision: require the actual agreed settlement date and its evidence; no inherited generic T+2 weekday default.

Start with fixed-rate or zero-coupon bonds quoted as a percentage of nominal, settling in the issue currency. The operator enters positive nominal, BUY/SELL, executed percentage quote, CLEAN or DIRTY, actual settlement date, total signed accrued interest for that nominal/date, and confirmation/source evidence covering both accrued interest and settlement eligibility. The administrator approves nominal minimum/increment and permitted OTC execution from instrument detail. The trade retains the approved bond terms and convention snapshot. Source documents, not free-text labels, remain the operational evidence.

For CLEAN: clean consideration = nominal × quote / 100; dirty consideration = clean consideration + accrued interest. For DIRTY: dirty consideration = nominal × quote / 100; clean consideration = dirty consideration − accrued interest. Accrued interest is in issue-currency units for the entire trade, not a percentage and not multiplied by nominal a second time. BUY means PAY, SELL means RECEIVE. These are gross amounts before fees, not confirmed cash postings. Both resulting amounts must be non-negative and exactly representable at supported database precision. Zero-coupon bonds require zero accrued interest.

## Deferred scope

Floating, inflation-linked, amortising, perpetual, yield-quoted and distressed/flat-price special cases; automated coupon schedules, day-count accrual, ex-coupon entitlement and final record-date rules; minor-unit/cross-currency settlement, lot allocation, fees/net invoice, settlement feeds, coupon/redemption events, positions, duration/DV01 and valuation/P&L. A manually supplied amount does not prove legal coupon entitlement or settlement confirmation. Missing or unsupported terms remain non-bookable. No historical share-shaped bond trade is silently reinterpreted.

## Delivered workflow and controls

Create and approve the bond using the existing bond instrument builder. From instrument detail, **Inspect / configure conventions** proposes sourced nominal minimum/increment and OTC permission. The existing Operations queue and tenant-administrator approval append an immutable BOND convention. For listed execution, **Add listing** now accepts bond parents through the existing approval workflow; currency and percentage divisor must agree with the booking convention. An ISIN does not choose the execution route.

Use **Book trade → Bond** to search the listed or OTC universe. Enter positive nominal, BUY/SELL, percentage quote, explicit CLEAN/DIRTY, total signed accrued interest, actual settlement date and settlement/accrual evidence. Existing parties, trader, tenant properties, tags and references remain available. Review/edit retains values. Confirmation uses the trusted tenant, an idempotency key and an execution-date terms fingerprint. History reads the typed clean/accrual/dirty breakdown from the original BOOKED event, not current master data. No position, payment or settlement event is posted.

Server checks require effective current approved instrument/listing definitions, one current effective fixed/zero-coupon bond terms record, positive par, redemption at par, same issue/quote currency, percentage quotes and future maturity. Fixed coupons require rate, frequency and day-count terms. Approved `principalBasis=UNADJUSTED_NOMINAL` is a sourced steward declaration: existing tables cannot automatically detect every amortisation/default special case. Do not approve the convention for an unsupported bond. Settlement evidence is an auditable operator reference, not an uploaded confirmation, calendar engine or independently verified entitlement.

New share-shaped cash booking of BOND is rejected on both client and server. Product P&L remains fail-closed for new bonds carrying a product BOOKED snapshot. Existing synthetic price-only bond fixtures keep their previous valuation behaviour; this is not a validation of real accrued-interest/coupon-aware P&L, and they are not silently reinterpreted as new product bookings. Wrong-product bond fields, futures intent on bonds, missing accrual/evidence, invalid nominal increments, settlement at/after maturity, zero-coupon nonzero accrual and negative resulting consideration are rejected. Accrual uses exact decimal arithmetic; dirty quotes are not accrued twice. Empty accrual is not silently zero. No purchase settlement default is applied.

Implementation: `security_master_schema/product_booking.py`; `apps/reference_api/product_booking{,_contracts,_routes}.py`; additive migration `20261002_58_bond_booking_conventions.py`; product ticket/model, convention form, `/api/trades/bonds`, booking navigation and history. OpenAPI and generated TypeScript contracts were regenerated. Migration 58 extends the convention kind check only; existing bond/trade columns are reused.
